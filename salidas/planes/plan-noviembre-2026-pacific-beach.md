## Seasonal context
- **Rainy season starts.** About 85% of San Diego's rainfall falls from November through March (NWS, sourced). November averages a high of 71°F, a low of 53°F and 1.2 in. of rain (NWS, sourced). This supports owner winter-prep and tenant leak-reporting content in weeks 1–2, before the first storms.
- **Fog and dry wind.** Fall and winter are usually the foggiest months, and dry easterly wind events happen mostly in fall (NWS, sourced). Good for atmosphere posts: foggy mornings on Crystal Pier, clear sunsets over Kate Sessions Park.
- **Off-season leasing.** Coastal leasing demand usually slows from November to January [NEEDS VERIFICATION: general pattern, no PB data]. - **Smaller academic turnover wave.** Winter-quarter and spring-semester turnover (Dec/Jan) can create a second, smaller leasing wave [NEEDS VERIFICATION]. Content mentions universities only as places ("close to UC San Diego"), never as types of residents.
- **Owner calendar.** Year-end review and 2027 rent-increase planning. The San Diego AB 1482 cap for Aug 2026–Jul 2027 (reported 8.2%) is **not officially confirmed** [NEEDS VERIFICATION]. No laws effective Jan 1, 2027 were confirmed [NEEDS VERIFICATION].
