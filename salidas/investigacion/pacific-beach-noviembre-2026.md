**Short-term rentals (STRO), and how they affect long-term rentals**
- City of San Diego STRO tiers: Tier 1 (rented 20 days or fewer per year), Tier 2 (home sharing, host lives onsite), Tier 3 (whole home outside Mission Beach, capped at 1% of housing units outside the Mission Beach community plan area), Tier 4 (Mission Beach whole home, capped at 30%). Tiers 3 and 4 have a 2-night minimum stay. [SOURCE: https://www.sandiego.gov/stro]
- License counts as of Oct 9, 2026: Tier 3 has 4,859 issued and 802 remaining; Tier 4 has 1,099 issued and 0 remaining; 8,429 issued in total. [SOURCE: https://www.sandiego.gov/stro]
- Operating an STRO without a license has been unlawful since May 1, 2023. Licenses expire after 2 years and may be renewed. Tier 3 and Tier 4 hosts must rent the home as an STRO for at least 90 days a year and file quarterly reports. [SOURCE: https://www.sandiego.gov/stro]
- An STRO is an occupancy of less than one month, measured from a calendar day to the same calendar day of the next month. Stays of one month or longer are not STROs, so mid-term furnished leases (for example, winter stays) fall outside STRO licensing. [SOURCE: https://www.sandiego.gov/stro]
- Pacific Beach falls under Tier 3, not Tier 4, because it is outside the Mission Beach community plan area. This is inferred from the tier definitions; confirm the boundaries with the city. [NEEDS VERIFICATION]
- Tier 3 fee approx. $1,170 ($41 application + $1,129 license), from a late-2025 third-party guide. [NEEDS VERIFICATION]

### 2. Owner topics

**California law**
- AB 1482 cap formula: 5% + regional CPI, maximum 10%. For increases effective on or after Aug 1, the April CPI applies. Source: search snippets; leginfo (Civ. Code §1947.12) blocked access. [NEEDS VERIFICATION]
- San Diego AB 1482 cap for Aug 1, 2026 to Jul 31, 2027: one property-management blog reports 8.2% (5% + 3.2% CPI), down from 8.8%, but cites no official source. Steadily (updated Aug 6, 2026) says the state had not confirmed San Diego's figure. A search snippet says the San Diego Housing Commission's form cites 3.2% CPI and an 8.2% maximum, but the page blocked access. Pages read: https://www.goodlifemgmt.com/blog/california-rent-cap-2026/ and https://www.steadily.com/blog/how-much-can-a-landlord-raise-rent-in-california [NEEDS VERIFICATION]
- The statewide CCPI cap for counties without a regional index is 8.6% (3.6% CPI). Steadily does not place San Diego in this group. [SOURCE: https://www.steadily.com/blog/how-much-can-a-landlord-raise-rent-in-california]
- Just-cause eviction rules (Tenant Protection Act; strengthened by SB 567 in 2024) and the AB 1482 exemptions (for example, housing built in the last 15 years, and certain single-family homes with the required notice) were not confirmed on an opened primary page. [NEEDS VERIFICATION]
- AB 12: since July 1, 2024, security deposits are capped at one month's rent, furnished or unfurnished. Small-landlord exception (up to 2 months' rent): the owner is a natural person, an LLC whose members are all natural persons, or a family trust, and owns no more than 2 rental properties with no more than 4 units in total. The exception does not apply to military service members. [SOURCE: https://caanet.org/legislation-limiting-security-deposits-signed-into-law-by-gov-newsom/]
- SB 611 (effective Jan 1, 2025) bans fees for serving or posting eviction, delinquency or vacate notices, and bans fees for accepting rent or deposits by check. From Apr 1, 2025: if a landlord charges a service member a deposit above one month's rent, the landlord must give a written statement before signing and refund the excess within 6 months. [SOURCE: https://journal.firsttuesday.us/new-law-takes-aim-at-junk-fees-from-california-landlords/95905/]
- AB 2801 photo documentation: move-out and after-repair photos since Apr 1, 2025, and move-in photos for tenancies starting on or after Jul 1, 2025. Photos must go with the itemized deduction statement. Pages read did not load; this comes from search snippets. [NEEDS VERIFICATION]
- Laws effective Jan 1, 2026: AB 628 (landlords must provide working stoves and refrigerators), AB 414 (expanded rules on returning security deposits on time), and AB 246 (eviction protection when nonpayment of rent is caused by delayed Social Security benefits). [SOURCE: https://www.csulb.edu/node/624411]
- Laws effective Jan 1, 2027: none found. The Governor's deadline to sign was Sep 30, 2026. A CalRHA report (Aug 2026) mentions SB 1296, a pet-policy disclosure bill with a proposed Apr 1, 2027 start, but I could not confirm it was signed. [NEEDS VERIFICATION]

**Winter weather and coastal maintenance**
- About 85% of San Diego's rainfall falls from November through March. [SOURCE: https://www.weather.gov/sgx/san-san]
- NWS San Diego climate summary for November: average high 71°F, average low 53°F, average precipitation 1.2 in. Fall and winter are usually the foggiest months. Dry easterly wind events, mostly in fall, can drop humidity below 20%. [SOURCE: https://www.weather.gov/sgx/san-san]
- Coastal winter maintenance practices (rinsing salt off metal fixtures and A/C condensers, checking gutters and downspouts before the first storm, roof and flashing inspection, caulking windows and doors, watching bathrooms and closets for mold and condensation, servicing water heaters and furnaces) are general best practice, not a cited source. Present them as guidance, not data. [NEEDS VERIFICATION]
