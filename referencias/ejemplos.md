# Model Listings

Two examples of well-written Grow listings. Use them as a reference for structure, tone and level of detail — not to copy word for word. All properties and data are fictional.

In both examples, every feature, descriptive adjective ("charming", "bright", "updated", "light-filled") and distance was given by the owner. Never reuse them for another property unless that property's data says so ("Descriptive words need data" and "Distance rule" in `marca.md`). "Ready for move-in" in Example 2 is used only because that home is available now: never write it (or "ready now", "move-in ready") when the available date is in the future.

## Example 1 — North Park bungalow (house)

**Headline:**
Charming 2 Bed / 1 Bath Craftsman with Private Yard in North Park

**Listing:**

Original hardwood floors, a sunny front porch and a fenced backyard — this 1920s Craftsman blends classic character with thoughtful updates.

**Home highlights**
- 2 bedrooms, 1 updated bathroom · approx. 950 sq ft
- Remodeled kitchen with quartz counters and stainless steel appliances
- In-unit washer and dryer
- Private fenced backyard with a covered patio
- Ceiling fans and new dual-pane windows
- 1 off-street parking space plus easy street parking

**The neighborhood**
The home is 3 blocks from the coffee shops, restaurants and breweries on 30th Street and University Avenue. The Thursday farmers market is 0.5 miles away, and Balboa Park is about 1.5 miles south.

**Lease details**
- Rent: $3,150/month
- Security deposit: $3,150
- Available: November 1
- Lease term: 12 months
- Pets: 1 dog or cat considered with additional deposit (assistance animals welcome per applicable law)
- Utilities: resident pays SDG&E; water, sewer and trash included
- Parking: 1 driveway space

**Why it works**
- The headline gives beds/baths, a unique feature and the neighborhood.
- The opening line sells the feeling in one sentence; facts follow in bullets.
- Neighborhood references are specific and real (30th Street, Balboa Park), with measurable distances the owner gave. Without a given distance, "close to Balboa Park" or "near Balboa Park" would be the right wording — never a drive time the owner did not give.
- It describes the home, not the "ideal tenant" — Fair Housing compliant.
- All key lease facts are easy to find.

## Example 2 — Pacific Beach condo (apartment)

**Headline:**
Bright 1 Bed / 1 Bath Condo with Balcony, 3 Blocks to the Beach in PB

**Listing:**

Catch the ocean breeze from your private balcony. The beach is 3 blocks away. This top-floor condo is light-filled, updated and ready for move-in.

**Home highlights**
- 1 bedroom, 1 bathroom · approx. 680 sq ft
- Top-floor corner home with vaulted ceilings
- Private balcony with partial ocean views
- Updated kitchen with breakfast bar
- Walk-in closet in the bedroom
- Shared laundry on site and secure bike storage
- 1 assigned garage space

**The neighborhood**
Crystal Pier and the boardwalk are 3 blocks west. Garnet Avenue's restaurants and shops are 0.4 miles away, and the Mission Bay bike paths are about 1 mile south. I-5 is close by for the drive downtown.

**Lease details**
- Rent: $2,650/month
- Security deposit: $2,650
- Available: Now
- Lease term: 12 months
- Pets: cats considered; no dogs per HOA rules (assistance animals welcome per applicable law)
- Utilities: resident pays SDG&E and internet; water and trash included
- Parking: 1 assigned garage space

**Apply online**
Tour this home and apply online at growpm.example/rentals or text (619) 555-0123.

*Grow Property Management · CA DRE #00000000 · Equal Housing Opportunity*

**Why it works**
- Every distance is measurable ("3 blocks", "0.4 miles", "about 1 mile") instead of "walking distance" or "steps from the sand".
- It uses sensory detail ("ocean breeze") without hype words.
- The HOA pet restriction is explained clearly and with the assistance animal note.
- The call to action is fast and specific.
