# Grow Property Management — Brand Guide

## 1. Brand essence

- **Name:** Grow Property Management (short form: Grow)
- **Tagline:** Local experts. Easy leasing.
- **Mission:** Make renting simple and stress-free for San Diego owners and residents.
- **Promise to owners:** "We treat your property like it's our own."
- **Promise to tenants:** "A great home, a fair process, and a team that answers."

## 2. Personality

| We are                     | We are not                  |
|----------------------------|-----------------------------|
| Professional and precise   | Cold or corporate           |
| Friendly and approachable  | Overly casual or slangy     |
| Proudly local              | Generic "any city" copy     |
| Confident                  | Pushy or salesy             |
| Honest and transparent     | Hype-driven or exaggerated  |

## 3. Voice and tone

**Voice (always the same):** professional, approachable, local.

**Tone (adjusts by audience):**

- **Owners:** calm, expert, results-focused. Use numbers and process when available.
  - ✅ "We market your home on 20+ rental sites and screen every applicant the same way."
  - ❌ "We're the BEST property managers in the universe!!!"
- **Tenants:** warm, inviting, practical. Paint the lifestyle, then give the facts.
  - ✅ "Morning coffee on the balcony, 0.3 miles from the bay."
  - ❌ "Don't miss this AMAZING once-in-a-lifetime deal!!!"

## 4. Writing style

- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Active voice ("We handle repairs" not "Repairs are handled").
- Use "you" and "we".
- Lead with the strongest benefit.
- Maximum one exclamation point per piece.
- No ALL CAPS words except abbreviations (A/C, HOA, DRE).
- Numbers as digits: "2 bed / 1 bath", "3 blocks".
- American English spelling.
- Emojis: allowed only on social media, max 2–3 per post, never in listings or emails to owners.

## 5. Preferred vocabulary

| Use                        | Avoid                          |
|----------------------------|--------------------------------|
| home, residence            | unit (in tenant-facing copy)   |
| residents                  | occupants                      |
| bright, updated, spacious (only when the user's data says so — see rule below) | stunning, breathtaking, dream  |
| 0.3 miles from, 3 blocks from (given distance); close to [PLACE], near [PLACE], nearby, close by (no distance given) | walk, walking (walking distance, a short walk to), steps from, quick (a quick drive), any time ("5 minutes", "minutes from") the user did not give (see section 7) |
| primary bedroom            | master bedroom                 |
| rental, lease              | "deal of the century"          |

**Descriptive words need data.** Use "bright", "updated", "spacious" — or any other descriptive adjective or feature — only when the user's data says so (the same word or a clear equivalent, e.g. "lots of natural light" → "bright"). Never add a feature or adjective that is not in the data (e.g. "bright", "updated", "in the building", "overlooking the pool"). Lifestyle imagery built on a real feature is fine: "morning coffee on your balcony" when the data lists a balcony.

## 6. Fair Housing — mandatory rules

Describe the **property and its features**, never the type of person who should live there.

**Never use (examples):**
- "Perfect for families", "great for couples", "ideal for singles"
- "Young professionals", "students only", "mature/adult community" (unless a legally qualified senior community)
- "No kids", "no Section 8", "no vouchers" (source of income is protected in California)
- "Great for active people"

Distance wording ("walking distance", "steps from"…) is covered by the distance rule in section 7, not by this list.
- "Exclusive", "private community", "safe neighborhood", "quiet neighbors"
- Any mention of religion, ethnicity, nationality, language, gender, disability, or age

**Always include in listings:**
- Equal Housing Opportunity statement or logo
- `Grow Property Management · CA DRE #00000000` (license from "Company constants" in `CLAUDE.md`)

**Pets vs. assistance animals:** if a listing says "no pets", add "(assistance animals welcome per applicable law)".

## 7. Distance rule

Describe location with **measurable distances**, never with phrases that depend on how someone gets around.

- **Only use distances the user gave.** Never take a distance from research, reference files, general knowledge or estimates.
- **Given distance:** "0.3 miles from the beach", "3 blocks from Crystal Pier". Drive times only when given, labeled and approximate: "about 15 minutes by car to downtown".
- **No distance given → allowed wording:** "close to [PLACE]", "near [PLACE]", "nearby", "close by" (e.g. "close to Fashion Valley", "near Balboa Park", "cafés and shops nearby"): no number, no placeholder. Never write `[X] miles`. **These four are correct wording, not banned phrases — never flag them.**
- **Banned:** "walk" and "walking" ("walking distance", "walkable", "a short walk to", "walk 3 blocks"), "steps from", "quick" ("a quick drive to", "a quick trip"), and any time ("5 minutes", "minutes from", "minutes away") unless the user gave it.
- **Fine:** "walk-in closet" (a feature) and "Ocean Front Walk" (a place name) are not distances.

## 8. Standard listing structure

1. **Headline** — beds/baths + top feature + neighborhood (max ~70 characters)
2. **Opening line** — the lifestyle hook in 1–2 sentences
3. **Home highlights** — 4–7 bullet points, real features only (see rule below)
4. **Neighborhood** — 2–3 sentences with real local references
5. **Lease details** — rent, deposit, available date, lease term, pets, utilities, parking
6. **Call to action** — how to schedule a showing / apply
7. **Compliance line** — Equal Housing Opportunity + DRE license

**Real features over bullet count.** "Never invent data" takes priority over "4–7 bullets".
- Home highlights contain only real features provided by the user (or confirmed in tagged research). Never create a bullet with a placeholder (no `[KITCHEN FEATURES]`, `[ADDITIONAL FEATURE]`, etc.).
- If there are fewer than 4 real features, write the ones that exist and ask for more features as a pending item (outside the draft — drafts contain only publishable text).
- Placeholders are used only for:
  - **Required lease data:** deposit, available date, lease term, pets, utilities.
  - **Square footage:** `[SQ FT]`.
- Distances never use placeholders (see "Distance rule").

## 9. Visual identity (reference for future agents)

- **Primary color:** Pacific Blue `#1F5F8B`
- **Secondary color:** Sand `#E9DCC3`
- **Accent:** Sunset Coral `#F26B4F`
- **Neutral:** Charcoal `#2E2E2E`, White `#FFFFFF`
- **Fonts:** headings in a clean sans-serif (e.g., Montserrat), body in an easy-to-read sans-serif (e.g., Open Sans)
- **Photography:** natural light, real San Diego settings, people shown diversely and in a natural way
