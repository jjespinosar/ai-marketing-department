# San Diego Neighborhoods — Reference Sheet

> ⚠️ General, approximate information for creative context only. This sheet has no distances or travel times on purpose: use only the distances the user gives for each property (see the distance rule in `marca.md`, section 7). Rent ranges are rough estimates and must be verified before publishing. Never use this sheet to describe who "should" live somewhere (Fair Housing).

---

## 1. North Park

- **Location:** Central San Diego, northeast of Balboa Park. ZIP 92104.
- **Character:** Dense urban neighborhood with Craftsman bungalows, Spanish-style homes and newer apartments. Known for its independent coffee shops, craft breweries, restaurants and arts scene.
- **Local references:** University Avenue and 30th Street corridors, North Park Observatory theater, North Park Thursday Market, Balboa Park (directly southwest).
- **Typical housing:** Small apartment buildings, condos, historic bungalows.
- **Commute:** Close to SR-163, I-805 and I-15; frequent bus lines (Rapid 215).
- **Approx. rents:** 1 bed ~$2,200–2,700 · 2 bed ~$2,900–3,600
- **Keywords for copy:** historic charm, dining scene, craft coffee, arts district.

## 2. Pacific Beach ("PB")

- **Location:** Coastal, north of Mission Bay. ZIP 92109.
- **Character:** Relaxed beach community with an active boardwalk, surf culture and a lively dining and nightlife area on Garnet Avenue.
- **Local references:** Crystal Pier, the Ocean Front Walk boardwalk, Kate Sessions Park (city and bay views), Mission Bay paths.
- **Typical housing:** Beach cottages, condos, duplexes, apartment complexes.
- **Commute:** Close to I-5.
- **Approx. rents:** 1 bed ~$2,300–2,900 · 2 bed ~$3,100–4,000
- **Keywords for copy:** close to the beach, ocean breeze, bike-friendly, sunset views, beach lifestyle.

## 3. La Jolla

- **Location:** Coastal, north of Pacific Beach. ZIP 92037.
- **Character:** Upscale seaside community with dramatic coastline, a village center of boutiques and restaurants, and major research and medical institutions in the neighborhood and just north of it.
- **Local references:** La Jolla Cove, Children's Pool seals, La Jolla Shores, Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve, UC San Diego, Scripps.
- **Typical housing:** Single-family homes, luxury condos, garden apartments.
- **Commute:** Near I-5 and the UC San Diego Blue Line trolley extension.
- **Approx. rents:** 1 bed ~$2,600–3,400 · 2 bed ~$3,600–5,000+
- **Keywords for copy:** coastal elegance, ocean views, village shopping, near UCSD and research campuses.

## 4. Hillcrest

- **Location:** Uptown San Diego, just north of Balboa Park. ZIP 92103.
- **Character:** Vibrant, diverse urban neighborhood with a strong community identity, restaurants, local shops and a major medical center.
- **Local references:** The Hillcrest sign, Hillcrest Farmers Market (Sundays), UC San Diego Medical Center – Hillcrest, Balboa Park (directly south), Mission Hills (directly west).
- **Typical housing:** Mid-rise apartments, condos, classic 1920s–1950s buildings.
- **Commute:** Next to SR-163 and I-8; close to downtown and the airport.
- **Approx. rents:** 1 bed ~$2,100–2,700 · 2 bed ~$2,800–3,500
- **Keywords for copy:** central, farmers market, near Balboa Park, close to hospitals.

## 5. Mission Valley

- **Location:** Central San Diego along the I-8 corridor and San Diego River. ZIPs 92108 / 92120.
- **Character:** Modern, convenient hub with newer apartment communities, major shopping centers and easy access to the rest of the city.
- **Local references:** Fashion Valley and Westfield Mission Valley malls, Snapdragon Stadium and SDSU Mission Valley River Park, San Diego River trail, Green Line trolley.
- **Typical housing:** Newer apartment complexes with amenities (pools, gyms, garages), condos, townhomes.
- **Commute:** At the junction of I-8, I-805, I-15 and SR-163; multiple trolley stations.
- **Approx. rents:** 1 bed ~$2,300–2,900 · 2 bed ~$3,000–3,800
- **Keywords for copy:** central location, resort-style amenities, trolley access, close to Fashion Valley, river trail.
